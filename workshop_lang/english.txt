In Build 42, felling a tree with the automatic [b]Cut down Tree[/b] action trains very little: vanilla gives 2 Strength XP per hit. This mod adds Strength, Fitness and Axe XP to every hit of that action.

[b]XP is granted once per hit, in single player and in multiplayer. Every value can be turned off or scaled in the sandbox options.[/b]

[h2]XP per hit[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Strength 4.8, Fitness 3.2:[/b] the same as one burpee rep. Vanilla's own 2 Strength XP per hit stays on top.
[*][b]Axe:[/b] in proportion to the tree health the hit removed, so every axe gives the same Axe XP per tree: about 20.7 for a 240-health tree. Per hit that is about 1.3 with a Hatchet, 3.0 with a Firefighter Axe and 4.8 with a Wood Axe.
[*]Only axe-category weapons give Axe XP.
[*]All values are before the server's XP multiplier.
[/list]

[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
The options are on the [b]Chop Tree XP[/b] page.
[table]
[tr][th]Option[/th][th]Default[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Strength XP from Chop Tree[/td][td]On[/td][td]Strength XP on each hit (4.8).[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Strength XP multiplier[/td][td]1.0[/td][td]0 to 100. Multiplies the mod's Strength XP.[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Axe XP from Chop Tree[/td][td]On[/td][td]Axe XP on each hit, by the tree health removed.[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Axe XP multiplier[/td][td]1.0[/td][td]0 to 100. Multiplies the mod's Axe XP.[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Fitness XP from Chop Tree[/td][td]On[/td][td]Fitness XP on each hit (3.2).[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
XP is granted where vanilla applies the hit: on the server in multiplayer, in the local game in single player. So each hit grants XP exactly once, and the client receives it through the normal XP sync. Add the mod to the server; every player needs it too.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Wraps the vanilla chop action without replacing vanilla files.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save.
[*]Do not use it together with other chop-tree XP mods, or the XP would stack.
[/list]
